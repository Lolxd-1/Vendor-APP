## Critical
1. src/screens/EarningsScreen.tsx:77-83,147-179 (with src/hooks/useRemote.ts:33,36) · Changing the week shows the previous week's money under the new week's label. `useRemote` deliberately keeps `data` when deps change (`setState(s => ({ ...s, loading: true }))`) and also keeps it on failure (`data: s.data`). EarningsScreen renders the summary and cards whenever `remote.data` is set, but builds the title, range and "Payout on …" line from the new `offset`. After pressing ‹, the screen shows "Last week · 5 Oct – 11 Oct · Payout on Mon, 12 Oct" with this week's Net receivable, Gross, To be paid and order cards until the up-to-20-page load finishes. If that load fails, the wrong week's numbers stay up for good with "<error>. Showing the last numbers." · Why it matters: these are payout figures attributed to the wrong settlement week. That is exactly what the requirement ("the earning should be proper and maintained") and E7 ("no half-loaded week shown as complete") rule out. Keeping stale data is fine for pull-to-refresh of the same week, but not across weeks. · Fix: tag each result with the week it belongs to, e.g. `getAllOrders(...).then(res => ({ ...res, offset }))`, and treat `remote.data?.offset !== offset` as having no data: spinner while loading, "Couldn't load earnings" + Try again on error. Another option is to remount the week body with `key={offset}`. Add an earnings.test case: resolve week 0, press "Previous week" with a pending (then rejected) promise, and assert '₹404.50' and 'ID: A' are not shown.
## Important
1. src/app/MainShell.tsx:124 (with src/screens/EarningsScreen.tsx:86-90) · `active={nav.tab === 'earnings' && !page}` makes `active` go false → true every time the vendor opens an order from Earnings and comes back. Each return calls `reload()`, which runs `getAllOrders` again: up to 20 sequential page requests. A vendor who checks 10 orders triggers 10 full re-fetches of the week. · Why it matters: D5 and AC8 limit Earnings loads to tab open, pull and week change because the backend is a sensitive production system. Returning from a detail page is none of those. The Insights pattern copied here never meets this case, because Insights opens no pages. · Fix: pass `active={nav.tab === 'earnings'}`. The tab is already hidden under the page by the `styles.hidden` wrapper, so the `!page` term is not needed. Add a shell.test (or earnings.test) case: open an order from Earnings, press Back, and assert `getAllOrders` was not called again.
