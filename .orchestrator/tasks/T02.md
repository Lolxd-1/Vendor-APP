## Test cases
- TC1 money: 245→'₹245'; 245.5→'₹245.50'; 12345→'₹12,345'; 123456.75→'₹1,23,456.75'; '12'→'--'; NaN→'--'.
- TC2 dayTime: same day 19:42 → 'Today, 7:42 pm'; previous day 00:05 → 'Yesterday, 12:05 am'; 3 Oct → '3 Oct, 7:42 pm'; null → '--'.
- TC3 minutesLabel: 0→'--'; 45→'45 sec'; 200→'3 min'; 3900→'1 h 5 min'; 'x'→'--'.
- TC4 paymentLabel: 'COD'/'cash'→'Cash on delivery'; 'UPI'→'Paid online'; undefined→'Payment not shown'.
- TC5 riderStatusLabel: undefined→'Finding a rider'; 'arrived_at_store'→'Rider at your shop'; 'PARTNER_ASSIGNED'→'Partner assigned'.
- TC6 historyStatusInfo('COMPLETED')→label 'Delivered', tone 'green'; ('weird_state')→'Weird state', 'neutral'.
- TC7 itemsSummary([{name:'A',itemCount:2},{name:'B',itemCount:1}])→'2 × A, 1 × B'; null→'No items'; initials('Tasty Bites')→'TB'; ''→'QV'.
- TC8 isClosedBySchedule: {}→false; Tue 00:00–00:00 on a Tuesday→true; Tue 14:00–16:00 at 15:00→true, at 16:01→false;
  Tue 22:00–02:00 at 23:00→true; Wed 01:00 with only Tue set→false. storeStatus(null)→{online:true,reason:null};
  manualOffline→{online:false,reason:'manual'}; closed by clock→{online:false,reason:'schedule'}.
- TC9 parseSchedule({manualOfflineOverride:true, operatingHoursJson:'{"Mon":{"openTime":"10:00","closeTime":"11:00"}}'})
  →{manualOffline:true, week:{Mon:{…}}}; operatingHoursJson 'not json'→week {}; null→{manualOffline:false, week:{}}.
- TC10 slotLabel(undefined)→'Open all day'; 00:00/00:00→'Closed all day'; 14:00/16:00→'Closed 2:00 pm – 4:00 pm';
  hhmmLabel('00:30')→'12:30 am'; shiftHHMM('23:30',30)→'00:00'; shiftHHMM('00:00',-30)→'23:30'.
- TC11 parseStats({response:{totalOrdersToday:5,revenueToday:1200,avgAcceptanceTimeSec:30,avgPreparationTimeSec:600,completionRate:80}})
  → {orders:5,revenue:1200,avgAcceptSec:30,avgPrepSec:600,completionRate:80}; totalOrdersToday 'x' → orders 0; {} → null.
- TC12 mergeOrders([{orderId:'A'}],[{orderId:'A'},{orderId:'B'}]) → ids ['A','B'], A is the first object.
- TC13 getOrdersPage('7',{statuses:['REJECTED','CANCELLED'],timeRange:'TODAY',page:2}) URL =
  'http://prd.quickverse.in/quickVerse/v2/order/7/orders?orderStatus=REJECTED&orderStatus=CANCELLED&timeRange=TODAY&page=2';
  statuses [] → no orderStatus key; returns {orders, totalPages} from body.response.
- TC14 getDashboardStats with {} body → rejects "Could not load today's numbers"; setManualOffline('7',true) → PATCH
  '/v1/vendor-schedules/shop/7/manual-offline?manualOfflineOverride=true'; saveSchedule('7',{Mon:{openTime:'10:00',closeTime:'11:00'}},true)
  → POST body {shopId:'7',manualOfflineOverride:true,scheduleActive:true,operatingHours:'{"Mon":{"openTime":"10:00","closeTime":"11:00"}}'}.
